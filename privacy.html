Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

The short version: we collect only what we need to sell you a cookbook and answer your emails. This website doesn't set cookies or run analytics or advertising trackers. We don't sell your information to anyone.

1. Who we are

Grandpa Harold Cooks ("we", "us") runs haroldcooks.com and sells the e-book Frugal Jewish Kitchen That Saves You Money. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and what choices you have. For questions, write to hello@haroldcooks.com.

2. What we collect

When you buy the Book. Checkout is handled by a third-party payment provider. You give them your name, email address, billing country or address and payment details. They pass us your name, email address, the country you bought from, what you bought, the price, and the order date. We never receive your full card number.

When you email us. We receive your email address, name if you include it, and whatever you choose to write.

When you visit the website. Like almost every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time. We use it only to keep the site running and secure. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser also sends your IP address to Google when a page loads; see Google's privacy policy.

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics tools, advertising pixels or social-media trackers.

3. How we use it

  • to deliver the Book and send your download link and receipt;
  • to answer your questions, fix download problems and handle refunds;
  • to keep records required for tax and accounting;
  • to prevent fraud and misuse, such as unauthorized sharing of the Book;
  • to tell you about a corrected or updated edition of the Book you bought.

We'll only send you marketing emails, such as news about a new book, if you've opted in, and every one will have an unsubscribe link.

If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, our legal bases are performing our contract with you (delivering what you bought), our legal obligations (tax records), our legitimate interests (running a secure website, preventing fraud, answering you), and your consent (marketing emails).

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only with:

  • our checkout and payment provider, to take payment and deliver the Book;
  • our website host and email provider, who store data on our behalf;
  • professional advisers such as an accountant, where needed;
  • authorities, if the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, they use recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, usually up to seven years. We keep emails for as long as needed to help you and then delete them in regular clean-ups. Hosting logs are kept only briefly.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, get a copy, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct their information and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms.

To use any of these rights, email hello@haroldcooks.com. We'll reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

The site is served over HTTPS, payments are handled by a dedicated payment provider, and we limit who can access order information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect what you share with us.

8. Children

The Book is sold to adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted here.
