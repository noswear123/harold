Refund Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

The short version: if the Book isn't right for your kitchen, email us within 30 days of buying it and we'll refund you in full. You don't need to give a reason.

Better you get your money back than a cookbook you don't use. — H.

1. 30-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund of the price you paid for Frugal Jewish Kitchen That Saves You Money for any reason within 30 days of your purchase date.

2. How to ask for a refund

Email hello@haroldcooks.com with the subject "Refund request" from the email address you used to buy the Book. Include your order number or receipt if you have it; if you don't, the purchase email address is usually enough for us to find it.

3. What happens next

  • We'll confirm your refund within 3 business days.
  • The money goes back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it can take 5–10 business days to appear.
  • You'll get the full amount you paid, including any sales tax or VAT collected at checkout.
  • After a refund, your license to use the Book ends, so please delete your copies and any printouts.

4. Download problems

If the file won't download or open, or a page looks wrong, write to us first. Nearly every problem can be fixed by sending a fresh link or a different copy of the file. You can still ask for a refund if we can't fix it.

5. Bought it twice by mistake?

Duplicate purchases are refunded in full at any time. Just let us know.

6. After 30 days

After 30 days, refunds are at our discretion, except where the law requires otherwise. If something has gone wrong, write to us anyway. We're reasonable people.

7. Chargebacks

Please email us before disputing a charge with your bank. It's quicker for you and saves both of us fees. We'll never make a genuine refund request difficult.

8. Your legal rights

This guarantee is in addition to any rights you have under consumer law where you live, and doesn't limit them. If you're in the UK or EU, you may be asked at checkout to agree that the download starts immediately. That ends the statutory 14-day cancellation period for digital content, but our 30-day guarantee still applies.
